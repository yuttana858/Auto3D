# Realistic corrugated board surfaces

Auto3D and the independent Packaging preview apply a restrained visual treatment automatically to supported generated boxes and extruded packaging profiles. Open Packaging → **Realistic board surface** for Enable, Panel bow, Fold softness, Cut compression and Paper grain. Individual materials remain editable in the Materials panel: Natural kraft liner (outer), Natural kraft inner liner, Exposed corrugated edge, and Wrapped closed score liner.

## Engineering separation

Canonical engine meshes, collision prisms, cut/score paths, dimensions and material calipers remain unchanged. Optional neutral `presentationMeshes` replace GPU position/normal/UV buffers only. Array spacing and size fields use original engine coordinates, not the bowed display bounds. Manufacturing exports and portable archive meshes retain canonical geometry; profile appearance settings restore visual buffers after loading. The audience mini viewer and independent preview use the same kernel. Mesh exports remain canonical rather than baking this display deformation.

`profile.appearance` stores validated dimensionless controls. Defaults are enabled, panelBow 0.18, foldSoftness 1, cutCompression 0.18, paperGrain 0.35. Strengths are 0–1 except foldSoftness (0–2). Settings and automatically generated texture changes share one Undo/Redo operation. Enable, bow, fold-softness and cut-compression changes update only the selected object’s display buffers. They preserve live fold previews and their pose, camera and textures; no full scene conversion or repeated fold-route audit is required. The whole asynchronous operation is locked against overlapping edits, including Undo/Redo. Paper grain remains an independent material property and continues to use the material refresh path. Turning geometry treatment off restores rigid panel buffers without flattening paper texture detail. Failed rendering restores the profile and materials. Invalid settings and read-only/changed-account edits are rejected. Custom print, normal, impression and varnish maps are retained; automatic grain updates replace recognized default paper maps and preserve their UV transforms. Shared materials follow the existing shared-material behavior across instances.

## Appearance approximations

Broad panel bow uses a smooth squared-sine envelope with zero displacement and slope at panel boundaries. A distance-to-boundary pinning envelope protects score connections. Sheet/flute direction introduces restrained deterministic variation. With t caliper and D the smaller panel span, its reference amplitude is min(0.22t, 0.006D) × panelBow. Both liners move together, retaining thickness away from compressed cuts. This is an appearance heuristic, not a constitutive paper model or a prediction of production bow.

Free cut boundaries receive a narrow quarter-circle shoulder with reference radius r = min(0.24t, 0.015D) × cutCompression. Within distance d < r, the face compression is r − sqrt(r² − (r−d)²). Inner and outer faces move toward the board centre. Recognized score rails are excluded from cut compression; existing circular score bridges keep separate compressed inner and expanded outer arcs. Fold softness scales their visual inner fillet. Edge-clustered tessellation and smooth face normals make these changes affect highlights and silhouettes. Visual compression does not change the manufacturing caliper.

Outer, inner and wrapped liner textures use deterministic periodic fibre noise and low-contrast mottling, with seamless borders. Their colour/roughness/normal/bump maps remain independent. Exposed edge texture repeats four flute waves per tile; global dieline UVs retain flute phase, pitch and direction while folding. The paper-grain control modifies generated paper detail; custom artwork and masks stay in the same UV coordinates as the deformed surface. Wrapped/closed means the liner around a known score, not a sealed manufacturer joint or a closed raw cut.

## Scope and limits

Generated 0200–0203 panels and custom extruded contours (including holes) have procedural surfaces. Custom flat extrusions receive a sheet-level bow; they are not automatically segmented into mechanically independent panels. Imported arbitrary meshes and generated boxes whose saved paths no longer match the supported rig retain canonical display geometry because reliable panel boundaries cannot be inferred. There is no flexible-body stress solver, individual flute crushing, fibre failure or converter-calibrated deformation model. Broad deformation is kept small to avoid padded/plastic appearance. Review representative materials and lighting before changing defaults.

Agent commands: `packaging.appearance.inspect` and `packaging.appearance.update`, using the same services as the panel. No publication or deployment occurs through these commands.

Crease triangles use a consistent material-sorted order in static and animated buffers, bounding each crease to three draw groups. Disabling and re-enabling restores/replaces GPU buffers and releases superseded geometry without changing canonical meshes.

Identical static array boxes share immutable GPU surface buffers within a scene conversion, rather than allocating a separate copy per box. An individual appearance edit receives private buffers so the other instances stay unchanged. `lighting.shadow.inspect` and the Error logs shadow review report geometry/texture/program/draw-call counts alongside context-loss and shader diagnostics.

## Continuous score bands and medium detail (Packaging 1.7.2)

Connected FEFCO scores reserve a finite band in the display mesh. Display triangles stop at the band's two rails and a matching bridge fills it, including in the flat pose. The outer and inner bridge faces use the adjoining liner material slots and interpolate original flat-sheet UVs; the old separate darker outer bridge is no longer used. The full engine panel remains unchanged for measurements, CAD, collision and manufacturing export. Turning board geometry off restores the original rigid display. Fold softness, board thickness, flute pitch and direction still affect the visual band/radius. Cut slots and the manufacturer's seam remain distinct from continuous scores.

`FlutePhaseAt` is shared by exposed-edge mapping and layered-medium geometry: phase = 2π(−x sin θ + y cos θ)/pitch. Edge UV U spans four pitches per tile, while V is board depth/caliper; PNG row direction is inverted when drawing the paper layers. The image's medium centre uses cosine of that same phase. Outer/inner liner and medium calipers set the section proportions. A cut parallel to the flute axis keeps constant phase; cross and oblique cuts traverse the phase field. The through-thickness cut faces of the actual layered specimen receive nondegenerate UVs.

Defaults use 256² liner/auxiliary maps and a 512² edge color map, with periodic fine fibres. Cached liner data depends on grain only; edge pixels additionally depend on normalized paper proportions. Closed-liner defaults share immutable outer-liner image buffers but retain independent editable mapping definitions. New FEFCO models assign outer/inner materials according to the erected box exterior/interior. Existing archived maps remain intact on load. Recognized default edge maps refresh when rebuilding after material-layer edits, while retaining UV adjustments; custom-named edge maps are preserved. Ordinary boxes still use a textured section rather than filling every flute with geometry. The separate cutaway specimen exposes actual textured medium geometry.

Review: `node test/browser/corrugate-review.mjs` renders a rounded joint, across-/along-flute cuts, and a medium cutaway into `work/texture-review/rendered-review.png`. This is an actual procedural-renderer capture, not a concept image.

## Closed/saved RSC regression (Packaging 1.7.3)

Closed RSCs need explicit coverage in addition to open-box close-ups. Top-fold and glue-tab rails can reverse triangle winding relative to the outward normals, causing two-sided material lighting to invert. Crease generation now checks the face cross-product against its outward normal and corrects the winding. Playback updates UVs with the corrected vertex order in both viewers.

Pre-1.7.2 saved crease meshes have no outer-liner GPU slot. The host presenter resolves missing liner bindings from the same object's panel materials before assigning new geometry groups. It appends references to existing GPU materials without altering engine geometry, source material records or saved archives. Repeated updates and shared geometry-cache hits retain the same slot count. The closed-RSC archive fixture contains both the old material slots and reversed top winding, and compares reopening with fresh generated data. This regression reproduces a host/material problem that an independent open-box render did not catch.
