# Auto3D 0.43.0 / Packaging 1.10.1

Double- and triple-wall exposed-edge previews show each medium separated by
an intermediate liner. Flute waves have continuous anti-aliased strokes and
different approximate periods, while remaining tileable in global sheet
coordinates. The catalog records total caliper and one pitch; the individual
wall heights, separator thicknesses and pitches in these maps are visual
approximations, not supplier-certified construction specifications.

The glued joint now uses a tighter visual score radius. The collision model
still reserves its original fold clearance, while a display-only transform
brings the glued flap to the shared hinge for the curved liner and exposed
edge. This applies throughout the fold preview and at generated endpoints;
canonical meshes, CAD paths, measurements and fold collision checks retain
their original geometry. Disabling the board-surface effect restores the
original rigid presentation.
