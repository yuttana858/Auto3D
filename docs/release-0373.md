# Auto3D 0.37.3 / Packaging 1.7.2

Corrugate material continuity: fold bridges share the adjacent outer/inner liner material slots and use a finite sheet-space UV band rather than stretching a single UV column. One flute-phase convention now drives exposed-edge UVs and the layered medium specimen. The edge color map uses selected outer-liner, inner-liner and medium paper calipers; these dimensions participate in the texture cache key. Across-, along- and oblique-flute cuts keep global sheet phase. Default liner and auxiliary maps increase to 256², with 512² exposed-edge color with finer periodic fibres; closed-score defaults match outer-liner color and roughness. Layered medium samples now carry the paper maps on their own surfaces. FEFCO facing roles are corrected to put the outer liner on the erected box exterior. CAD coordinates, solids and dimensions are unchanged; score-band geometry remains a visual overlay, not calibrated crease mechanics.

These are new procedural defaults. Existing archived maps remain preserved on load; recognized default edge maps refresh during material rebuilds with UV edits retained, while custom-named edge maps are preserved; creating a new box uses the updated texture set. A saved box can use its existing outer material continuously across the new fold bridge without replacing artwork. The exposed edge remains a cross-section texture in ordinary box rendering; the separate layered specimen has actual medium geometry. Display-panel triangles are clipped once per rig at the score bands; arrays continue to share cached display buffers. No medium geometry is added to every box.

Review images come from `node test/browser/corrugate-review.mjs`. Full validation is recorded in project-status.md.
