## Visual and physical limits

These are presentation models with adjustable outside dimensions and nominal wall thickness. Inner offsets at seams, shoulders and small radii are not a production shelling solver; positive volume and closed topology do not certify wall uniformity, self-intersection freedom, capacity or closure fit. Caps and clamshells remain closed, without mating threads or opening articulation. Pull tabs are visible geometry, not an operating scored opening.

Natural HDPE translucency and amber glass are approximated with transmission, roughness and tint. There is no calibrated subsurface scattering or spectral absorption. Pearl plastic is a satin metallic approximation, not an interference shader. Small deterministic procedural maps provide material grain without external assets; they are not scanned, measured PBR materials. Lighting/environment strongly affect their appearance.

Label finishes affect the whole label patch. Clear gloss film preserves alpha from PNG/WebP art; it does not infer background removal from an opaque image. Foil uses the artwork colors as the metal base. CAD SVG/text/vector conversion remains paused. The local prototype checkpoint is `feat/primary-cad-labels` at `c7ae486`; it is not part of this PR or deployed.

## Follow-up build gates after this increment

1. **Measured shape profiles:** author additional generic shoulder/heel/panel profiles, true flip-top seam/latch geometry, pump/dropper variants, food-can scores, clamshell ribs/dividers and handle/hang-hole options. Validate each against licensed drawings and measured outside/cavity dimensions; keep source dimensions separate from illustrative presets.
2. **Surface quality:** use an approved reference lighting rig and compare clear PET, frosted glass, HDPE, aluminum, kraft film and molded fiber. Add licensed scanned textures if they measurably improve close-ups; track physical scale, source license and mobile memory. Investigate absorption/subsurface support before claiming calibrated translucency.
3. **Flexible packaging:** seam-width controls, localized film wrinkles and fill profiles that preserve zipper/spout clearance. Check labels across gussets, seams and deformation. Keep responsive interactive rendering with a bounded mesh budget.
4. **Print finishes:** dedicated spot varnish/foil/emboss artwork layers and previews with explicit substrate versus ink behavior. Reuse the team's finalized CAD document/layer IDs and coordinate contract when available; do not introduce a competing layer schema.
5. **Engineering:** introduce a validated offset/solid kernel before capacity, manufacturing wall thickness, closure interference or Boolean-solid guarantees. Keep visual geometry and engineering certification distinct in the UI.

Resizing acceptance: preserve outside dimensions, object identity, body finish and physical texture density; optionally scale label size/offsets; validate all changed values before mutation; one Undo step and portable save/reopen.
