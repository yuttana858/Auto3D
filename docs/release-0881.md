# Auto3D 0.88.1 — Auto Pack compact help

The Auto Pack explanation is collapsed under How Auto Pack works. The packed contents controls are renamed Pads and Partitions and are separately collapsible. Settings and sizing behavior are unchanged. The fold progress indicator is now a draggable fold-pose timeline, available after automatic playback; it saves the pose through the existing undoable fold scrub. The Auto panel no longer shows pause/cancel buttons or repeated explanatory status. `packaging.auto.explanation` and `packaging.auto.contents_panel` control the sections.
