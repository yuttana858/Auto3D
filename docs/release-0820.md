Convert CAD shows an animated progress bar during preparation, private upload, queueing, reading, geometry construction, validation, meshing and publication. Upload bytes, native face counts and acknowledged artifact counts provide **current-stage percentages**, not an invented estimate of the entire conversion's remaining time. Unknown work remains indeterminate. The bar reaches a completed job only after native validation and all required files publish. It includes an elapsed timer, reduced-motion support and accessible progress values; polling updates existing nodes and resumes after busy actions instead of freezing.

The worker now distinguishes unsupported Parasolid schemas/entities, incomplete or malformed sources, resource limits and unexpected native failures. An isolated child writes only bounded phase/counter telemetry. Server-only failure diagnostics contain an exception category and code-file/line locations, never exception text, source lines, local variables or CAD geometry. Public job records expose only fixed error codes and safe progress.

Older generic failures and unexpected worker failures can retry once through the existing private pipeline. Two attempts remain the total limit; retries recheck owner/global queue capacity and receive a fresh processing deadline bounded by the original 24-hour retention. Known unsupported schemas or geometry cannot loop through Retry. Sources stay immutable and private; no customer CAD enters the repository, images or logs.
